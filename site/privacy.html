Privacy

How I handle your information

People hand me personal documents for a living. Here is what I collect, why, and what I do with it.

What I collect

  • When you book. Your name, email, phone number if you give it, the meeting place or address, and what you tell me about your documents. The booking calendar is run by Calendly, which stores these details for me, and the appointment appears on my Google Calendar.
  • When you call, text or email. Whatever you choose to send me.
  • At the appointment. Utah law requires me to record each notarization in my notary journal. An entry includes the date and time, the type of notarization, a description of the document, your printed name, signature and address, details of the ID you showed me, and the fee.

What I don't do

  • I don't sell, rent or trade your information, and I don't add you to a marketing list.
  • I don't keep copies or photos of your ID.
  • I don't keep copies of your documents once the appointment is finished. For a loan signing, the documents go back to the title company or lender the way they instruct.

Who sees it

I do. Beyond that, I share information only in two cases: with the title company, lender or signing service that arranged a loan signing, and when the law requires it, such as a lawful request to inspect my notary journal.

How long I keep it

  • My notary journal stays in a secure place. Utah law requires me to keep it for 10 years after its last entry and then destroy it.
  • Booking details and messages are kept only as long as I need them for your appointment and my business records.

This website

This site has no advertising trackers and no visitor analytics. Three outside services help it run: Calendly provides the booking calendar, Google provides the fonts, and Vercel hosts the pages. Each receives basic technical details, such as your IP address, when a page loads, and Calendly may set cookies when you use the calendar. Calendly explains its practices in its privacy notice.

Your requests

You can ask what information I have about you, or ask me to correct or delete it. The one exception is my notary journal, which the law requires me to keep. Call or text (801) 350-1609 or email collectivheartfelt@gmail.com.
