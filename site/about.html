About me

Hi, I'm Beth.

I'm a mobile notary public and loan signing agent in Midvale, Utah, and I'm bilingual in English and Spanish.

When you book with me, I'm the one who shows up. I'll check your ID, make sure every signature and date is where it belongs, and take the time to get it right. If Spanish is easier for you or for someone in your family, we can do the whole appointment in Spanish.

A little background: I have a master's degree and studied statistics at the graduate level, so careful, detailed work is familiar ground for me. I'm an NNA certified signing agent, I've been background screened, and I carry errors and omissions insurance. I also run a small business here in Midvale, so I know what it means when someone trusts you with their time.

Outside of work I'm a mom, a daughter and a dog lover, and I care about this community because it's mine too. The people who know me would call me punctual, responsible and caring. I'd like the chance to show you.
